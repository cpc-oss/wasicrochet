About us

A tiny studio, a lot of love

Crochet Petal Craft began with a single crochet hook, a basket of pastel yarn and a love for small, beautiful things.

Every clip, band and flower you see here is made by hand in our home studio. There are no machines and no factory runs — which means each piece carries tiny, human variations that make it one of a kind.

We work in small batches so we can pay attention to every stitch, choose soft skin-friendly yarns, and finish each piece so it is comfortable to wear all day.

This website exists so you can see everything we make in one place, at your own pace. When something catches your eye, message us on Instagram and we will take it from there — personally.

Because everything is handmade, colours may vary very slightly from photographs and sizes can differ by a few millimetres. We think that is part of the charm.

What we make

  • Crochet Hair Clips — Delicate petal clips and pins for everyday and festive wear.
  • Hair Bands — Soft crochet bands and headbands that stay comfortable all day.
  • Scrunchies — Gentle, no-crease crochet scrunchies in pastel shades.
  • Crochet Flowers — Everlasting handmade blooms, bouquets and single stems.
  • Kids' Accessories — Tiny, soft and cheerful pieces made for little ones.
  • Gift Sets — Curated handmade bundles, ready to gift.
  • Hair Accessories — Delicate, playful and charming accessories made to add a sweet touch to every little hairstyle.

Why this website exists

Sharing dozens of photographs with every customer on Instagram was slow for us and confusing for you. This catalogue keeps every design in one calm place, always up to date, so you can browse at your own pace and message us only when you have found the one.
