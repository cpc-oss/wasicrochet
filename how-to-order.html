Simple & personal

How to order

No cart, no checkout, no card details on this site. Just four steps and a DM.

Browse the collection

Explore the full catalogue and pick the pieces you love.

Note the product code

Every product has a code such as CPC-HC-001. Tap it to copy.

Open Instagram

Use the “Order via Instagram” button on the product.

Send us a DM

Share the product code, quantity and colour. We confirm availability, price and delivery.

What to include in your DM

  • The product code (for example CPC-HC-001)
  • Quantity you need
  • Preferred colour, if the design is customisable
  • Your city or PIN code for delivery charges
  • The date you need it by

What happens next

  • We confirm availability and making time
  • We share the final price including shipping
  • You confirm and complete payment as agreed on DM
  • We make, pack and dispatch, then share tracking

Reading the labels

  • Available — ready to dispatch
  • Made to Order — crocheted after confirmation
  • Limited Pieces — only a few left
  • Sold Out — kept visible, may return
  • Coming Soon — launching shortly
There is no cart or payment page on this website. We will never ask for card details in a DM — payment happens only through the method we agree with you directly.

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Pick your product here, copy its product code, then tap “Order via Instagram” and send us the code in a DM. We confirm availability, final price and delivery.

Why can't I buy directly on the website?

Everything is handmade in small quantities, so availability changes daily. Confirming on Instagram means you never pay for something that is out of stock.

Can I get a different colour?

Often yes. Products marked “Customisable” can be made in other shades, subject to yarn availability. Ask us on DM.

What does “Made to Order” mean?

That design is crocheted freshly after you confirm the order. It takes a little longer, but you get to choose the colour.

Do you ship outside your city?

Yes, we ship across India. Charges and timelines are confirmed on DM before payment.

Are these safe for children?

We use soft, skin-friendly yarn. However, small crochet accessories are not toys — please supervise young children.
