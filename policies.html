Good to know

Shipping, care & policies

Everything is handmade in small batches, so a few things work a little differently here.

Shipping & dispatch

  • Orders are confirmed over Instagram DM before dispatch.
  • Ready-to-ship items are usually dispatched within 2–4 working days of confirmation.
  • Made-to-order and custom items need additional making time, which we confirm on DM.
  • Shipping charges and delivery timelines depend on your location and are shared on DM before you pay.

Care instructions

  • Hand wash gently in cold water with a mild detergent. Do not machine wash.
  • Do not wring or twist. Press between a towel to remove excess water.
  • Dry flat in shade. Direct sunlight can fade the yarn colour.
  • Keep away from hooks, velcro and rough surfaces to avoid snagging.
  • Store flat in a dry pouch. Do not iron directly on the crochet work.

Returns & exchanges

  • Because every item is handmade in small batches, we do not offer returns for change of mind.
  • If your item arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, message us on Instagram within 48 hours of delivery with unboxing photographs and we will make it right.
  • Custom and personalised orders cannot be cancelled once making has begun.
  • Slight variation in colour, size and stitch placement is normal for handmade products and is not considered a defect.

Privacy

  • This website is a static catalogue. It has no accounts, no cart, no payment page and no tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We do not collect, store or share any visitor information on this site.
  • Any details you share with us (name, address, phone) are used only to make and deliver your order.
  • Conversations happen on Instagram and are subject to Instagram's own privacy policy.
